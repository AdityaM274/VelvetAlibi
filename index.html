The rules of a good alibi

Move with arrows or WASD, or use the on-screen arrows. E leaves one double on your tile; walk away, then X trades places with it. R recalls it from anywhere. Your double holds brass switches and stops an usher’s red sightline. Matching curtains stay open while either of you holds their switch. Only you can collect tickets and exit. Unsafe moves are refused with an explanation; they cost no turns. Undo and hints have no penalty. Some acts begin with a double already on stage. Switches are lowercase a/b; matching curtains are uppercase A/B. The lit rim marks the real performer; the dashed blue rim marks the double. No timer or saved progress.
